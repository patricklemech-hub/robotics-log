# robotics-log

This repository tracks my roadmap from complete beginner to building a working robot arm. It's also a public statement of my commitment to break into the robotics field.

## About Me

I immigrated from Vietnam in 2018. From my first steps in the U.S., I felt this was the turning point in my life — the land of opportunity, but also full of challenges. Since then, I've worked across many fields: cook, nail technician, delivery driver, retail worker. Through all of it, I always felt something was missing — I kept wondering what my purpose was, and what I could do for the next generation.

The reason I want to become an engineer isn't just about building a better life in the U.S. It's a dream I've had since I was a child: to do something meaningful, something that contributes to society and humankind. I'm not sure I'll become the person who changes the world — but I'm sure I'll give it my best try.

### End of the intro

If you are reading this log, you are witnessing the life of one person who has the will and eagerness to achieve the goal of becoming a good engineer. Thank you, and see you out there.
